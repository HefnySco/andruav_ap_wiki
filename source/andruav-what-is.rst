=================
What is Andruav ?
=================


.. youtube:: ak4IFExkYsA

|

Andruav makes an Android phone into the companion computer of your drone,
boat or rover. It is the companion computer that everybody already owns.

One phone replaces many parts: the telemetry radio, the FPV camera and
transmitter, and the GPS logger. You get :ref:`telemetry with no range limit
<andruav-telemetry>` and live video over Wi-Fi and 3G/4G/5G.

Andruav runs only on the vehicle. You monitor and control the vehicle from
the :ref:`webclient-whatis` in a browser, on a computer or on a phone.
Andruav is not a ground station app.

Since version 15, Andruav runs the real DroneEngage modules inside the phone.
See :ref:`andruav-de-native`.



.. tip::

      Download Andruav from `here <https://cloud.ardupilot.org/downloads/Android/latest/>`_



The phone on the vehicle connects to the `ArduPilot <https://ardupilot.org/>`_
flight controller ("FCB"). It sends telemetry and receives commands over the
internet. It uses the phone camera for FPV video and photos.
