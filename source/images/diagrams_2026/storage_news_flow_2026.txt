INTENDED CONTENT: news flow. The admin (storage dashboard, global or account
scope) or a WebClient user ("Post News" panel, account scope only) posts news.
The Storage Server saves it (SQLite) and pushes it at once (mt 9018) to the
Communication Server(s), which deliver it to the WebClients of the account,
or of all accounts for global news.
