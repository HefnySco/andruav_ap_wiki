INTENDED CONTENT — what this diagram should show:

Two hardware inputs on the left:
  - "MI48 IR Sensor" (serial connection)
  - "RGB Camera" (/dev/videoN)
Both feed arrows into:
  - "de_ir_camera" module (orange box) — labeled: fuses IR + RGB, detects
    hot/cold point.

de_ir_camera's output feeds into a dashed "Virtual Video Drivers
(v4l2loopback)" band containing three virtual-device boxes in sequence,
each consumed by the next real module below/beside it:
  - "DE-THERMAL" (virtual device, output of de_ir_camera)
    -> consumed by "de_tracker" (tracks the hot/cold point)
  - "DE-TRK" (virtual device, output of de_tracker)
    -> consumed by "de_ai / GCS" (reads DE-TRK)

Separately, de_ir_camera also sends a message directly to the comm bus
(sidecar box, dashed arrow down from de_ir_camera): "Comm Bus -> GCS",
labeled with TYPE_AndruavMessage_TrackingTargetLocation (1043), noting
this message ID is REUSED for unrelated hot/cold thermal point
reporting (normally it's the tracker's own {x,y} offset message).

Legend (top-left): orange = DE-Module, dashed yellow = Virtual Video
Device, gray = Hardware Input.
